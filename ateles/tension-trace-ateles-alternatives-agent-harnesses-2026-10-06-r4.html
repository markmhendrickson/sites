Before choosing

Try the contract you need.

These are evaluation questions, not reported pass/fail results.

  1. Assign work with a principal, action scope and required approval. Change or revoke the grant before the effect.
  2. Inspect every relevant adapter, kill and restart the worker, and verify retry ownership, spend limits and recovery.
  3. Confirm the result against the exact artifact, decision and external read-back. A passing trace is distinct from action enforcement.
