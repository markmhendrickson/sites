Explore the concept

Every task keeps its reason.

An ask becomes accountable work when its intended outcome, relevant context and dependencies are explicit.

A follow-up is not just “send something.” Its work record identifies the result sought, the criteria for acceptance and the context needed to proceed.

Mission, strategy, project and plan explain why the task matters. A workflow explains how contributors advance it. Purpose ancestry is not a five-step execution route.

Example

A meeting commitment becomes a follow-up with a clear result.

01Make the commitment concrete.

Record the meeting commitment as one task with an intended result and acceptance criteria.

02Keep its purpose attached.

Relate the task to the relevant plan and project, so the next contribution knows what outcome it serves.

Reference intake is configured; the full canonical lifecycle is still in development.
