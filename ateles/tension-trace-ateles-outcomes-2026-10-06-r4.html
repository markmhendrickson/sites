Explore the concept

An assurance is not an outcome.

The work is inspectable through its contributions, decisions and evidence. Completion concerns the result—not merely the attempt.

An article review belongs to a particular revision. A later change needs its own applicable review; the earlier verdict is not a blank cheque.

After publication, inspect the actual destination. Authorization permits action; a successful request reports an attempt; confirmation supplies evidence of the resulting effect.

Example

Publish the approved article—and check its destination.
