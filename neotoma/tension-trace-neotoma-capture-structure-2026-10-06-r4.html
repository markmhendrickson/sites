Capture + structure

Keep the email. Connect its records.

An invoice email and its attachment are originals. The invoice and contact extracted from them are related records, each with its own identity.

Start with the source.Give each thing an identity.See the agent interaction

01 · Capture

Start with the source.

Your agent reads the selected invoice email and retains its body separately from invoice.pdf. It records the invoice and contact it extracts without treating that interpretation as the original message.

1Keep the email and PDF separately.

Retain the supplied invoice email and invoice.pdf as distinct originals.

2Read the extracted facts back.

Check invoice AS-1042: EUR 1,200 due 22 October, with the contact and supporting email.

02 · Structure

Give each thing an identity.

Invoice AS-1042 belongs to one identified invoice record. The sender is a separate contact. A named relationship connects those records; source evidence supports individual field values.

1Identify the invoice and contact.

Keep invoice AS-1042 as one invoice record, separate from the sender contact.

2Name the relationship.

Relate the invoice to the contact. A relationship between entities is not a source citation.
